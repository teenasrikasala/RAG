About Campus Guide

Campus Guide helps students find information about attendance, examinations, hostel rules, leave policies and student conduct.

It uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to retrieve relevant text from college documents and generate an answer using Llama 3.

Answers depend on the available documents. Please verify important decisions with your college office.
